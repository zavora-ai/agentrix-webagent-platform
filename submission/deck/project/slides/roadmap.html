Brick 1 is the single-node build: one site, end to end, on Vultr. Brick 2 adds payments through the Effects Gateway, with PayPal and M-Pesa, each payment an approved and receipted effect. The pilot, December to January, takes ten sites onto the fleet profile on VKE, with the brief and approvals on WhatsApp. Version 1 in the first quarter of 2027 adds the agency console, the Vultr Marketplace listing, billing and more regions. Zavora starts in Kenya, so M-Pesa and WhatsApp come first.
