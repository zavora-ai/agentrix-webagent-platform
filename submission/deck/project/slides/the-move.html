The Move has seven steps. The four in dashed boxes run in sealed rooms on the sandbox VM. Control plans the jobs, signs each job spec, dispatches it to a room, and gets back artifacts by digest, which it checks and receipts. Both of Challenge 1’s patterns are here: Pattern A is the builder writing page code that a room builds and renders, with errors fed back; Pattern B is a browser in a room, a vision check of parity, and the owner’s approval before go-live. Real owners upload their WordPress export on the Move page. The footer times are from the first live Move, 50 pages on Phase 1 (Claude) routes. Undo is one step on the Move page for 30 days: back to the previous release or the old site, receipted.
