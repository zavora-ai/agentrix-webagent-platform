Agentrix WebAgent Platform · identity proposal · 7 Nov 2026

A WebAgent is a living mark

Each WebAgent gets a mark that is unique to it, generated from its identity and dressed in the site's own brand. The owner can tune how it looks. What each motion means stays fixed by the platform, so a visitor learns it once and it never lies. There is no face.

Three sites, three teams

Same platform, same grammar of states. Each site's WebAgents share a silhouette and a pattern family, so they read as one team, and each one's seed makes it unique. The Chief WebAgent's mark carries the team as dots, which pulse while that WebAgent works.

States change on a demo timer here. On a live site each change comes from a presence frame, never from a model call.

Four layers

The first three are the owner's to brand. The fourth belongs to the platform.

Silhouette
A squircle whose corner softness starts from the site's own corner radius, read at the Move. It never becomes a circle, because circles mean a person, such as the owner's chip when Wanjiru joins a chat.
Signature
A pattern generated from the site, the WebAgent and a seed the owner can shuffle. The site picks one family so its WebAgents look related; the seed makes each one unique. It shows from 28 px up, never stronger than 30% over the fill, and moves only while the WebAgent is working.
Glyph
The role as a line icon (cake, loaf, newspaper), or a monogram set in the site's display face.
State
The halo, the trace line, the amber dot and the grey wash. These mean the same thing on every site and in the console (06 §4.3, 07 §3.5).

Ten states, one grammar

      
Shown on the mark you are tuning. The owner's words and the visitor's words come from 06 §4.3 and 07 §3.5. With reduced motion, every animation stops and each state still reads by its shape. Without it, motion settles after 5 seconds in one state.

    

    

  


  

    
Who decides what

    

      

        
The owner chooses

        
    
          
  • Colour, from the site's palette or any colour that keeps 3:1 against the site background.
    • 
          
  • Corner softness, within the squircle range.
    • 
          
  • The glyph: a role icon, a monogram in the site's display face, or later the owner's own drawing.
    • 
          
  • The signature family for the whole site, and a fresh seed for any WebAgent.
    • 
          
  • The name on the menu.
    • 
        

      

      

        
The platform keeps

        
    
          
  • What each motion means. Ten states, the same on every site and in the console.
    • 
          
  • No face, eyes, human name or photo, and no circles, because those mean a person.
    • 
          
  • The AI label beside every mark a visitor sees.
    • 
          
  • Contrast of 3:1 for the mark against the site, and for the glyph against the mark with its pattern drawn.
    • 
          
  • Stillness when idle. Motion appears only while something is really happening.
    • 
        

      

    

  


  

    

      
One mark wherever it works

      
The same spec renders everywhere, so the Cakes desk a visitor met on the site is recognisably the one that sends the owner a WhatsApp card.

    

    

      
On the site
Desks, peeks and threads, the agent door page, and link previews of shared artifacts. The mark wears the site's tokens from the Move.

      
In the console
The dock, WebAgent cards and decision cards, wearing Agentrix tokens around the mark. The Chief WebAgent's orb is its team mark.

      
Off the site
WhatsApp briefs and cards as a static image padded for round crops; the A2A agent card's icon; MCP server and tool icons. Voice uses the speaking ring, driven by the real voice level.

    

  


  

    
How it is built

    

      

        
    
          
  • A small MarkSpec lives in the WebAgent's spec. Changing it publishes with the next release, like any page change.
    • 
          
  • One generator, two builds. Rust renders static SVG into the signed release and PNG for image-only channels; the console runs the same algorithm for live preview. Golden tests keep them identical.
    • 
          
  • Deterministic. The pattern comes from a seeded generator keyed on the site, the WebAgent and the seed, so a mark looks the same everywhere and can be rebuilt from its spec.
    • 
        

      

      

        
    
          
  • State is one attribute. Presence frames set data-state; all motion is CSS on transform and opacity.
    • 
          
  • Small. The size of the mark you are tuning is shown under its spec.
    • 
          
  • Uploaded drawings are hostile input. When owners can upload a glyph, it is cleaned in a sealed room into plain single-colour paths, with no scripts, links or embedded images.
    •
