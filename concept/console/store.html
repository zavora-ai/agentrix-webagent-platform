Permissions are effect classes

A listing's "can" list is its tools grouped by effect: read, draft, send, publish, schedule, money. Anything that reaches outside your site starts at Ask me first. A tool whose declaration changes after you added it asks again before every call.

Adding is a charter amendment

The sheet compiles into one charter change: the new WebAgent, its jobs, their starting levels and its budget. Your passkey signs that change; without one, a single-use code from your authenticator app approves it. Adding a WebAgent never raises another WebAgent's authority, and missions can't either.

Packs are signed and roll out in waves

A pack teaches an existing WebAgent a job (a playbook, a capability, sometimes a desk). Packs are reviewed and signed by Zavora, roll out to a few sites first, and roll back automatically if evaluations regress.
