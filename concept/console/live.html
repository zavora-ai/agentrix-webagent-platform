The wall still holds

The owner is a person on a paired device, so you may read and join Front of house conversations. Suggestions during a takeover come from the desk that already reads the thread. The back office only receives the typed signal it would have received anyway.

Honest labels

Visitors always see who is speaking: "Cakes · AI" or "Wanjiru · a person". Joining and handing back are shown in the thread. Presence words come from the tool that is actually running.

Voice is for talking, not signing

By voice you can ask, deny, pause and stop everything, which acts at once. Money, publishing and anything high-risk always open the approval sheet on a screen, for your passkey or a code.
