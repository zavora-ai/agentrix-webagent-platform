Flour on your hands, a customer at the counter, and a go-live card waiting. Six hand gestures run the shell. A gesture can move, open, snooze, switch worlds, stop everything, or bring up the passkey sheet. It can never approve.

Turn on gestures on this device?

  • The camera is read only on this device. No frame is uploaded or stored: a small hand-tracking model in this browser turns each frame into 21 points per hand, and the frame is dropped.
  • Plain rules, not an AI model, decide which of six hand shapes it is. You hold each one until a ring fills.
  • A gesture can never approve. Approving always needs your passkey, or a code from your authenticator app.
  • The camera turns off after 2 minutes without a hand, when you leave the tab, after a Freeze, and during quiet hours. The green pill shows whenever it is on.

Your browser asks for camera permission next. In this concept the hand tracker (MediaPipe Tasks Vision 0.10.14) loads from jsDelivr and its model from Google's model store, both pinned. Those hosts see a download, never a frame. The product serves both itself.

Calibration · about 60 seconds, on this device only

Calibrate gestures for your hands and your lighting

01 · Light and framing

Checks that your hand is found in the gesture zone. Advice comes in words: "Too dark, face the window."

02 · Which hand

Left or right, and whether the image is mirrored, so a swipe always goes the way you mean.

03 · Each gesture, 3 times

Records your finger-curl and thumb angles and your swipe speed. Movement is measured by hand size, so your distance from the camera matters less.

04 · Just talk, 10 seconds

Talk with your hands as you normally would. If any gesture would have fired, its threshold rises, and we tell you which one.

Stored in this browser as numbers only, with no images. Recalibrate or reset from the camera pill. Not built in this concept yet: it uses the default thresholds in gestures.js.

Tested against recorded clips

Targets, measured before anything is claimed.

The corpus is recorded hand-landmark tracks, never video. It covers at least 10 people, four lighting conditions, distances from 0.4 to 1.5 m, a laptop and a tablet, and an hour of everyday background: talking with hands, typing, a mug, a phone call, handling goods, someone walking behind. It runs in CI on every change.

The safety line

Gestures run shell verbs and two server commands, stop everything and pause one WebAgent. Approving needs a real tap on "Use passkey" and the passkey's user verification, or a code typed from your authenticator app, and no hand shape can produce any of them.

Accessible by design

Gestures are optional and never the only way to do anything. Every one has a key, a tap and a word. Hold times can be lengthened, and reduced motion shows a static progress bar.
