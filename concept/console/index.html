The owner console: your WebAgents, your decisions and your site in one place

Agentrix OS won its hackathon on one idea: an operating system where everything is an agent. For a website owner, the agents are the site's WebAgents: a front desk that talks to every visitor, an editor, a sales lead, a guardian. They work in the background while the owner sleeps, live while customers are on the site, and only ever with the authority the owner signed.

The big ideas

Nine ideas behind the console

1 · The Chief WebAgent is the shell

One command bar, text or voice. It plans, shows what will happen and what it will cost before anything starts, delegates to WebAgents, and answers in one voice. It is not an app, has no tools of its own that reach outside, and has no human name.

2 · WebAgents are apps

A dock with live presence, a Store with plain-words listings, permissions grouped by effect, authority per job, a budget per app, pause and remove. Adding one is a charter change you sign.

3 · Two worlds are two walls

Front of house (visitors and AI agents, live, untrusted) and Back office (your WebAgents at work). The shell shows the wall between them, and the only things that cross it are typed signals.

4 · Night Time

The shell follows your opening hours. During quiet hours it turns dark, only emergencies reach you, and the field becomes a timeline of the work. In the morning the timeline becomes your brief.

5 · Missions

Say a goal in your own words. The Chief WebAgent turns it into a contract: what counts, who works on it, when they wake, the budget and the end date. You sign it, and it reports every Monday, including what didn't work.

6 · Sit in, take over, hand back

Watch any visitor conversation unseen, step in as yourself, then hand it back. While you type, the desk whispers facts from your own pages. What you say can become an answer for everyone.

7 · Walk the site by voice

Open your own site and talk. The Chief WebAgent points at what it means, Editor drafts the change, and you sign the release. Voice can stop everything; it can never sign.

8 · Every decision is bound to its effect

Your passkey signs the exact effect (or, without one, a single-use code from your authenticator app approves it): amount, recipient, release. That fingerprint runs once and becomes a receipt anyone can verify. One switch freezes everything, from the app, WhatsApp or voice.

9 · Hands-free, safely

Six hand gestures for floury hands, recognised on the device with nothing uploaded, each held until a ring fills. A gesture can stop everything or bring up the passkey sheet, but it can never approve.

Spatial or calm?

On a phone it is a short list. On a desk it shows more.

Agentrix OS was spatial and expressive. The platform chose a calm, phone-first console. Both are right, for different moments, so the console keeps one model and draws it two ways.

What makes it an OS

A shell agent, apps with permissions, processes that sleep and wake, a task manager, a notification policy, a kill switch, and a memory you can read. These are the same on every surface. The desk view draws them spatially: dock, field, lane, worlds, Night Time.

What keeps it calm

The phone draws the same model as a short stack: brief, decisions, live, apps. There are no gestures to learn and nothing to arrange, and the lock screen says only that something is waiting. Most mornings take two minutes, and quiet hours are really quiet.
