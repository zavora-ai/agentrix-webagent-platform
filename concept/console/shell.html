Your WebAgents, what they are doing, and what needs you

The Chief WebAgent is the shell: you ask, it plans, the WebAgents do the work, and it answers in one voice. WebAgents sit in the dock like apps. The two worlds are the two halves of the security design: Front of house, where visitors and other AI agents talk to your site live, and Back office, where your WebAgents work. During opening hours the shell is in Day Time; after closing it turns to Night Time.

Two worlds are two partitions

Agentrix OS split Work and Home so neither saw the other's data. Here the split is the security design itself: Front of house reads untrusted text and holds no privileged tool; Back office acts and never reads raw text. The shell shows the wall and every typed signal that crosses it.

Day Time and Night Time

The shell follows the business's opening hours. During quiet hours it turns to the dark Agentrix OS field, the lane goes silent except for emergencies, and the field becomes a timeline of what the WebAgents did. In the morning it becomes the brief.

The Chief WebAgent is the shell, not an app

It owns no tools that reach the outside world. It plans, delegates to WebAgents, asks one question when it must, and shows the plan, the decisions it will create and the cost before anything starts. It has no human name and no face.

Many sites, one OS

Several sites, or a whole portfolio: one shell

An owner with several sites switches with the site chip, or opens "All sites". An agency gets the same shell in its Portfolio scope. Either way, each decision is still made on its own site's card, under that site's charter, and an agency acts only where the owner granted it a role.
