The other rails

The fleet release

M-Pesa STK Push

For a Kenyan M-Pesa number. M-Pesa sends a payment prompt to the buyer's phone, for example KSh 2,550 to Kilele Bakery. She enters her PIN on her own phone.

It charges shillings, so no exchange rate is needed.

The site never sees the PIN.

Card provider-hosted fields

The card fields on Kilele's pay page are hosted by the card payment provider. What she types goes straight to the provider.

The card number never reaches Kilele, its servers or its desks.

M-Pesa needs a Kenyan phone number. Njeri, in London, pays with PayPal.

Underneath

  1. Njeri Picks "Pay with PayPal" on kilele-bakery.example, after seeing the amount, the payee and the rate.
  2. Effects Gateway Holds Kilele's PayPal keys. It creates a PayPal order (Orders v2) for the exact amount, payable to Kilele Bakery, under the order's fingerprint H(site, webagent, tool, canonical_args, amount, expiry). For K-1043 this happens only after Wanjiru approves, and only for the amount she approved.
  3. PayPal Njeri signs in and approves on PayPal's own page.
  4. Effects Gateway Captures once. The fingerprint is the idempotency key, so a double click or a retry can't charge her twice.
  5. Effects Gateway Writes a receipt: the order, the amount, the pinned rate, the payment method and PayPal's reference.
  6. Desks See "Paid" and the amount. They never see a credential.

Dollars on PayPal, and why

PayPal's list of supported currencies has no Kenyan shilling, so PayPal charges US dollars. The rate is the one Kilele posts in its price list, 1 USD = KSh 129.00, pinned on each order. A refund uses the same rate.

Seen before PayPal opens

The amount, the payee and the rate are on Kilele's own page first. Payment requests appear only on her orders page at kilele-bakery.example, so a link in a message can't change where she pays.

A trusted surface

The purchase completes on PayPal's own page, or on her own phone for M-Pesa (06 §12.4). Desks quote and request. They never take a payment.

Never on Kilele

Her PayPal sign-in, an M-Pesa PIN and a card number stay with the provider. The Effects Gateway holds Kilele's PayPal keys, and no desk can read them.

Fees are Kilele's

PayPal's fee is the merchant's cost. It is illustrative, isn't added to Njeri's total and isn't shown to her. Whether Kilele's PayPal account can take these payments and withdraw to a Kenyan bank is checked with PayPal before the pilot.
