Nothing is charged until go-live

The plan and the subscription are approved now. The first charge is on go-live day, and an owner who never goes live is never charged (08 §4.1).

One door for money

  • The Effects Gateway creates, revises and cancels the PayPal subscription. Each change is an effect with a receipt.
  • PayPal collects each period. Verified webhooks post each payment to the ledger against its invoice.
  • Overage and other one-off charges never go on the subscription; they take Steward's governed path (08 §4.2).

The budget is a limit, not a meter

Wanjiru sets the cap. AI above the included amount is billed only up to the cap she chose, at cost plus a disclosed margin. Raising the cap later is a decision card. Prices are hypotheses (01 §6).

Never

  • Card or PayPal credentials in the platform
  • A charge she didn't approve
  • Two prices for the same thing
