What happens next

  1. Wanjiru gets the order card on her phone.
  2. She approves with her passkey, or with a code from her authenticator app if she has no passkey.
  3. The payment request appears on your orders page at kilele-bakery.example: USD 40.70 to Kilele Bakery.
  4. Nothing is charged until you pay it there.

If Wanjiru suggests a change or declines, you'll see it on the same page.

Numbers come from rules

Prices come from the price list, availability from the bake plan, and slots and the fee from the delivery calendar. No model sets, rounds or converts a number (d-exp-rules-price). The dollar figure is the price list's posted rate, applied by a rule.

A quote that holds

Both quotes are pinned to the release they were built from, with a valid-until (06 §5.1). If a price changes tomorrow, these quotes don't move.

An honest no

The bake box has no gluten-free option, so Orders says so, cites the menu and offers what does exist. A desk never invents an option or guesses an allergen.

Held, and nothing charged

request_custom_order is a held call. No order is confirmed, no payment request exists and nothing is charged until Wanjiru approves: with her passkey, or without one, an authenticator code bound to this order.

Desks see typed fields

The design travels as a brief artifact with its digest. Wanjiru's card shows the design. Back-office desks see only its typed fields (tiers, sponge, finish, message, note, date, area), never Njeri's account, sign-in or payment details (06 §5.4, §4.10).

The first release ends the custom order at Wanjiru's approval. Paying for either order is step 4, which is the fleet release (12 Nov).
