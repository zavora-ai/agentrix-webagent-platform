If her assistant carries a mandate Pilot · AP2

Some assistants can carry an AP2 mandate: Njeri's signed say-so for one purchase. Then four signed records hang together, and none of them can be reused on its own.

  1. 1

    Njeri's mandate

    Njeri signs, on her own device

    She tells her assistant what it may buy, and signs it. The mandate names one checkout by its hash: a short fingerprint of exactly what Kilele offered.

    mandate → hash(checkout)
  2. 2

    Kilele's signed checkout

    Kilele signs

    The checkout lists the lines, USD 19.77, the payee and an expiry. Its hash becomes the subject of the order's canonical arguments: the exact inputs the order will run with.

    canonical_args.subject = hash(checkout)
  3. 3

    Wanjiru's approval

    Wanjiru approves, passkey or code

    She signs one fingerprint over the site, the WebAgent, the tool, those arguments, the amount and an expiry. So it covers this checkout, on this site, until it expires, and nothing else. The same fingerprint is the idempotency key, so the order runs once.

    H(site, webagent, tool, canonical_args, amount, expiry)
  4. 4

    The receipt

    The Effects Gateway records it

    The receipt records the whole chain and how Wanjiru approved. Anyone can check it with awp verify.

Why a chain and not one hash: the mandate carries Njeri's authority and the approval carries Wanjiru's, and both are needed. Binding the site and the expiry stops an approval being reused on another site, or after it runs out. AP2 v0.2 support is upstream work U24, planned for the pilot.

Found through the AI Catalog

The assistant reads /.well-known/ai-catalog.json. One file lists the MCP tools at /mcp, the signed A2A card, the UCP profile and the agent terms. No one has measured how often assistants find these files unaided, so the pages stay plain and readable too (06 §12).

Known, not verified

A valid Web Bot Auth signature earns known. It proves who runs the agent, not who the user is. verified and partner come only from lists Wanjiru keeps. Agents that don't sign arrive anonymous, and the terms say what they can't do.

Never a payment credential

The door returns a checkout link on kilele-bakery.example. No tool takes or returns a card, a PayPal login or anything else that can pay. Purchase stays on trusted surfaces: Kilele's page, PayPal's page and Njeri's phone.

Numbers from rules

The quote comes from the price list, stock and the delivery calendar, pinned to price list v14 with a "valid until". No model sets a number, at the door or on the site.

A hold the agent can't lift

request_custom_order is a held call. On A2A it's a task still working, with a status saying it waits for the owner; on MCP it's a Task. Agents poll until push arrives (U4). Only Wanjiru's approval, checked on the server, releases it.
