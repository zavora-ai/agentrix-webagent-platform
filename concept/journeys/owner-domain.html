Prove kilele-bakery.example is yours

Public records show it's registered with a Kenyan registrar, and your old host serves the site. Pick whichever is easier.

  1. Sign in to your registrar and open the DNS page for kilele-bakery.example. Agentrix never asks for that password.
  2. Add a TXT record. Name: _agentrix Value: agentrix-site-verification=k1l3-7f2c-91aa
  3. Save it, come back, and press Check. Some registrars take a few minutes.
  1. Download agentrix-verify.txt. It holds only a code.
  2. Upload it with your old host's file manager so it opens at https://kilele-bakery.example/.well-known/agentrix-verify.txt
  3. Come back and press Check.

Next: the Move starts

Proof, not passwords

The Caretaker checks a public DNS record or a public file. The proof is recorded in the Trust log with the time and method. Agentrix never asks for the registrar or hosting password (01, rung 2).

Nothing moves yet

The proof only lets the Mover start. The DNS switch is part of the go-live card, which Wanjiru approves with her passkey or a code (step 6).

When it doesn't work

The Caretaker says what it saw in words: "No TXT record yet. Your registrar may take up to an hour." It never guesses, and it doesn't retry in a loop.
