Changes wait for Wanjiru

A new date is held for her OK, unless she has signed a standing rule that covers it. Njeri sees "Waiting for Wanjiru's OK", and nothing changes until then.

A rule with four limits

The Cashier refunds alone only under Wanjiru's signed rule:

  • up to KSh 2,000 per order;
  • only to the original payment method;
  • at most 5 a week;
  • until 31 Dec 2026.

The Effects Gateway checks her signature and every limit before the refund runs. It refuses a rule that lacks any of the four (ADR-012).

Back the way she paid

Refunds go only to the original payment method, here her PayPal, at the rate pinned on the order. KSh 1,350 at 129.00 is USD 10.47.

One cap on value given

Refunds, discounts, store credit and free items share one cap for the site. When it's reached, even a small refund waits for Wanjiru, so giving can't move from one channel to another.

No promise it can't keep

Above the rule, the desk says who decides and by when. It never says "you'll get your money back" before Wanjiru has approved it.

Receipts anyone can check

A payment receipt is signed by the Effects Gateway, which took the payment; every issuer's key is listed at /.well-known/agentrix/keys. Tap Verify, or run agentrix audit verify --receipt. (awp verify is a different check: whether a live page is an approved release.) PayPal's receipt is its own, sent separately.
