Only when needed

Asking, designing and sharing by link need no account. Ordering, saving a design or tracking an order asks, and says why. The desk links to this page, and the thread resumes afterwards (06 §4.10).

A passkey first, and why

A passkey can't be typed into the wrong site. It is bound to kilele-bakery.example, so a look-alike can't use it (W3C WebAuthn). Kilele stores only its public key, so there is no password to leak.

MFA is always on without one

Email and password, Google, LinkedIn or GitHub: each adds an authenticator code. There are no magic links. Social sign-in asks for a name and a verified email only, and Wanjiru picks which providers appear. Decided by James, 7 Nov 2026 (d-auth-passkey-first).

The honest cost

Many bakery customers don't have an authenticator app. Passkeys first and one passkey offer per device reduce the hurdle, and 06 §9 measures drop-off at the MFA step. If people leave there, the open question is whether visitor accounts may rely on the provider's own MFA (06 §4.10).

Desks see "customer"

Desks see the entitlement and what Njeri chose to share. Never her credentials, how she signs in, or a social profile.

Never

  • A desk asking for a password, code or card in a chat
  • Device memory merged into her account without her confirming each item
  • An extra choice switched on for her
  • A card number or PayPal password kept by Kilele
