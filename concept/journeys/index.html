Two journeys, one order

Wanjiru moves Kilele Bakery onto the platform. Njeri, in London, orders a birthday cake from it. Each lane is a set of screens; the amber points are where the two lanes meet: the order card, the payment, the payout and a refund.

The figures (illustrative)

  • K-1042: 2 sourdough loaves (KSh 450 each), a weekend bake box (KSh 1,350), delivery (KSh 300). KSh 2,550 = USD 19.77.
  • K-1043: Amina's 2-tier forest cake, KSh 5,250 = USD 40.70.
  • Kilele's rate: 1 USD = KSh 129.00, posted in the price list and pinned on each order. PayPal doesn't settle in shillings.
  • Delivery: Sat 14 Nov, 10:00–12:00, Kilimani.

What never crosses the lanes

  • Njeri's passkey, password, PayPal login, or card number. Desks see "customer", not the account.
  • Any number a model made up. Prices, dates and stock come from rules.
  • A payment request Wanjiru hasn't approved.
