What stops it

A new device needs proof

Her passkey, or the password plus an authenticator code. A stolen password alone gets nowhere.

Big changes need a fresh sign-in

Changing the email, phone or delivery address asks her to sign in again and tells the old email. A thief can't quietly redirect her account or her cakes.

Desks can't change account details

No chat can be talked into it. The desk points to the account page instead (06 §4.10).

Sign out everywhere

Ends every session, this device included. She signs back in with her passkey.

What stops it

Passkeys are scoped

A passkey works only on the site it was made for (W3C WebAuthn). On kilele-bakeri.example it simply isn't there.

Real desks never ask

Kilele's desks never ask for a password, a code or a card (06 §4.10). A chat that does is not Kilele.

Reports reach two places

"Report a look-alike" goes to Wanjiru and to the operator.

The honest limit

A password can still be typed into a look-alike, and a fast one can pass a code on too. That is why the passkey comes first, and why the new-sign-in alert and "Sign out everywhere" exist.

What makes it true

One path, every store

Every store that can hold her data is registered. The request completes only when each one has answered with a receipt (ADR-028).

On a clock

Account memory goes within an hour (06 §9); everything else within 30 days (ADR-028). The receipt shows each store's time.

What the law keeps, said plainly

Orders stay, reduced to what the law requires, and the receipt says which. Payment records are pseudonymised, not deleted.

Backups don't bring her back

Backups are never edited. The erasure ledger is replayed before any restore, so nothing returns.

Calm and specific

Each screen says what happened, what Kilele has already done, and one next step. Nothing shouts, and nothing counts down.

One domain, one place to pay

Kilele's emails and messages link only to kilele-bakery.example. Payment requests appear only on her orders page there.

Nothing to give away in a chat

Desks never ask for a password, a code or a card, and can't change account details. They see "customer", not the account (06 §4.10).

Reports go somewhere

"Report it" reaches Wanjiru. "Report a look-alike" reaches Wanjiru and the operator.

Never

  • A link from Kilele to any other domain
  • A payment request changed after approval without Wanjiru approving again
  • A desk asking for a password, code or card
  • An erasure receipt that hides what was kept
