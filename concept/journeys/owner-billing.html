Invoices, approved and posted

The Billing WebAgent drafts each run, and finance approves it with a passkey or an authenticator code. Invoices post to zavora-era and go through eTIMS. PayPal collects each period, and verified webhooks post each payment against its invoice (08 §4.2).

Plan changes are effects

The Effects Gateway revises or cancels the PayPal subscription, and every change has a receipt. Overage and one-off charges never go on the subscription.

Late payment

Reminders on day 0, 3 and 7. Read-only from day 14, with the last signed release still serving. A closure notice with an export link on day 30. A person decides each suspension (08 §4.2).

Never

  • Hold her site or her data hostage
  • Bill AI above the cap she set
  • Delete without notice and a receipt
