Experience 03 · Agentic search

Search that answers from the shop's own pages.

The search box understands a request rather than matching keywords. It shows how it read the question and lets you edit that reading. It answers with sources from this shop's own pages and offers the next step. When a plain lookup is better, it simply finds the product. Plain results are always one tap away and work without a model.

Try:
classyperfumes.shop/search

What I used

Three speeds of search

  1. <50 msSuggestions while typing, from the release's static indexno model
  2. instantNavigational queries ("rain oud") go straight to the productno model
  3. <1.5 sRequests ("a gift under…") stream an answer with citationsmodel
  4. <100 msEditing the reading re-ranks the catalogue by rulesno model

Why it beats the old box

  • It shows its reading. "≤ KSh 5,000 · vanilla · a gift" are chips the visitor can correct, so a misread is visible and fixable.
  • Honest fit, not just matches. "Over budget by KSh 1,400" and "Not vanilla" are said plainly.
  • Answers lead to actions: add to bag, compare, write the gift card. Comparisons save as a shareable artifact.
  • Gaps teach the shop. "Is it vegan?" goes to the owner's "teach your site" list.

Search never

  • Invents a product attribute the catalogue doesn't hold (longevity, vegan, "similar to" another brand)
  • Ranks by margin while claiming to rank by fit
  • Hides the plain results or the filters
  • Writes an essay when the visitor typed a product name
