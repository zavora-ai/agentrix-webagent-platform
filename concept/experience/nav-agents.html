Experience 01 · Navigation is the team

The menu talks back.

On a migrated site every menu item is a WebAgent. Hover or focus one to meet it: what it does, what it knows right now, and three things to ask. Start typing and the conversation happens in place. Click the label and it is still an ordinary link to an instant, static page.

Try: hover Cakes · Tab to a menu item and press ↓ · press / to ask the whole site · on a phone, tap Menu.

  6. <1.5 sFirst words of a desk-scoped answer via Vultr Inference (<1.0 s if cached)model

How a WebAgent shows it is working

A WebAgent is a mark in the site's own colours with its menu name. No face, no human name, no typing dots. Its state is always true: "working" appears only while a tool is really running.

What the menu never does

  • Open a chat on its own, or pop up on arrival
  • Call a model because the pointer passed over it
  • Break the link: the label always goes to the static page
  • Promise a price, date or allergen the site doesn't state
  • Pretend to be Wanjiru. A person is one tap away, with an honest wait time
