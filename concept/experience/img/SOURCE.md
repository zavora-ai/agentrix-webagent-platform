# Image source

`amber.jpg`, `citrus.jpg`, `rain.jpg` and `vanilla.jpg` are generated product renders for the
Classy Perfumes concept. They are not photographs of real products.

- **Origin:** Zavora's Agentrix Website proof of concept, `concept/img/`. They were added by
  James Karanja in commit `4af02f6` (30 Oct 2026, "generated photography"). That repository is
  Apache-2.0, © Zavora Technologies Ltd.
- **Reuse:** reused here with disclosure, under James's decision that proof-of-concept work may
  be reused in the platform, and relicensed MIT by the copyright holder.
- **Changes:** resized to 900 px wide and recompressed for these concept pages.
- **Not recorded:** which image model generated them.

The concept pages label them "Concept render". In line with
[docs/06-website-experience.md](../../../docs/06-website-experience.md) §10, a real site would
never present a generated image as the product.
