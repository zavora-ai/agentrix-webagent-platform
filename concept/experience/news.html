Archetype · National news publisher

A news site whose desks answer from published stories

The Kestrel Post is a fictional national publisher with thousands of stories a week. After migration its sections become desks: WebAgents that answer only from published, dated, bylined reporting, link every claim, and never write news. Readers can catch up since their last visit, follow a story with its corrections, and set up a digest they control. The front page stays static and instant.

Try: Catch me up · hover the Transport desk · set up a digest on the right.

House rules for desks
Desks never write news. They answer from published reporting.
Every claim links to a dated, bylined story.
Subscriber stories: headline and link only, unless you subscribe.
Corrections reach answers and digests within minutes.
Editors approve each desk's scope and wording.

Why a publisher moves

  • Readers get answers from the archive, with links back to the stories. Years of reporting answer "what happened since" with links, so readers return to the publisher, not to a third-party summary.
  • Corrections propagate. An answer or digest that cited the old figure is flagged, an integrity feature print never had.
  • Desks drive subscriptions honestly. Locked stories appear as headline and link, never paraphrased.
  • Same truth for AI assistants through the agent door, with attribution and entitlements intact.

Memory without tracking

"Since Tuesday" and "your topics" are kept on this device after the reader taps "Remember". They are sent only with a "Catch me up" request, shown as chips, and treated as untrusted input. The site stores nothing and assigns no identifier. Signed-in readers can opt into memory that follows them across devices.

Device memory is settled with the architecture (S2): items are schema-checked, never count as identity or consent, and are off by default for government, health and education sites.

Desks never

  • Generate a news story, a quote or a headline
  • Answer from unpublished, embargoed or subscriber text for non-subscribers
  • Drop the byline or the date from a claim
  • Personalise what counts as news. Topics filter; editors rank
