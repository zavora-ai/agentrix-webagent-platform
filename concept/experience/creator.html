Experience 02 · Make it with me

Design the cake together.

The Cakes WebAgent turns "she loves strawberries and the forest" into a cake Kilele actually makes. It asks one thing at a time and says plainly what isn't offered. The sketch is composed from the bakery's own options and the price comes from its price list, so the model never sets a number. Wanjiru confirms every order.

Try: Play Amina's 7th, or pick options yourself, or type something like "make it pink with flowers for 30 people".

    The model's only job is mapping words to the options the owner approved ("forest" → green ombré; "strawberries" → fresh berries) and saying honestly what isn't offered. The configurable-product schema holds the options, constraints, lead times and price deltas. Totals come from rules in under 100 ms, as a quote pinned to the release.

    Arrives on WhatsApp as "A decision is waiting". Approving opens her passkey on her phone, or asks for an authenticator code if she has no passkey. The design link is attached.

    The studio never

    • Shows a generated photo as if it were the product
    • Offers an option, size or date the bakery hasn't approved
    • Guesses allergens or "safe for coeliacs"
    • Takes payment before Wanjiru confirms
    • Pushes upsells. Extras appear only when the visitor asks for them
