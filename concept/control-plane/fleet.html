One shell, three scopes

The operator console uses the owner console's tokens, decision card and approval sheet: passkey first, an authenticator code without one. The indigo scope mark and the switcher say you are acting on the fleet, not on one business's site.

08-control-plane §7.1

WebAgents propose; people decide

Operator WebAgents pull only reversible levers inside standing grants, such as capping a background queue. Infrastructure applies, promotions past ring 1 and anything with money need a person's approval, by passkey or authenticator code: one approver or two, never fewer.

08-control-plane §5.2–5.3

Content-free by construction

Fleet views carry ids, counts, durations and money, never conversations or drafts. Every operator action on a site also lands in that site's Trust centre, in plain words.
