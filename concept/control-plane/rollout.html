08-control-plane §3.5

Stopping is cheap; going on is signed

The Release WebAgent may halt and roll back on its own. Ring 1 needs one approver; rings 2 to 4 need two different people, as the operator charter's approval chain requires.

08-control-plane §5.3

Behaviour changes need the owner

Platform internals and security patches roll under the operator's charter. A pack upgrade that adds capabilities, a playbook revision or a theme change becomes the owner's decision.
