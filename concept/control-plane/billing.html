One subscription per site

Each site's own Vultr Inference subscription is both its isolation and its meter. The gateway reserves and settles every call; Vultr's usage is the check. Disputed numbers are never billed.

08-control-plane §4.2, 02-architecture §6

Money moves through one door

M-Pesa, Paystack and PayPal via Steward run only in the Effects Gateway, against a signed approval of the exact payment. Payouts need two signers. No WebAgent sees a credential.

08-control-plane §4.2, ADR-004

Zavora's books on Zavora's ERP

Invoices and payments post to zavora-era's double-entry ledger, which transmits Kenyan invoices to KRA eTIMS. Amos, the ERP's accountant, reconciles; a person clicks for every ledger posting.
