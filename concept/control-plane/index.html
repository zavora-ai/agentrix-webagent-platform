Agentrix WebAgent Platform · the control plane · by Zavora

Zavora runs on Zavora.

How Zavora, and any agency or reseller, runs all its customers, its infrastructure on Vultr and the business itself. The operator's own work is done by WebAgents, through the same governed path, gateways and sandboxes as a bakery's, under people who decide with passkeys (an authenticator code without one). Engineers and agencies change it with Zavora CLI, and every change rides a release train.

The operator's own team

Operator WebAgents, under human authority.

They live in Zavora's operator tenant: their own kernel, charter, budget, gateways and receipts. They hold no charter role, so they propose, investigate, draft and pull only reversible levers. They never read a site's content.
