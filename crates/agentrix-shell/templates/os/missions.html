How a mission starts

01 · You say it

"Get us 60 Christmas cake orders by 18 December." Typed here. Voice and WhatsApp can't start one yet.

02 · The contract

Your Chief WebAgent drafts what counts, who's named on it and when it ends. You check it, and it becomes a decision you read before anything starts.

03 · You approve it

With your passkey, or a code from your authenticator app. The receipt names the contract.

04 · It reports in

Its count and pace show here and on Today. Pause or end it with one tap.

What a mission does today: it counts progress from your site's records. The WebAgents named on it don't yet change what they do to reach it.
