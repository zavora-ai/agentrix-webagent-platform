Turn on gestures on this device?

  • The camera is read only on this device. No frame is uploaded or stored: a small hand tracker in this browser turns each frame into 21 points per hand, and the frame is dropped.
  • Plain rules, not an AI model, decide which of six hand shapes it is. You hold each one until a ring fills.
  • A gesture can never approve. Approving always needs your passkey, or a code from your authenticator app.
  • The camera turns off after 2 minutes without a hand, when you leave the tab and after a Freeze. The green pill shows whenever it is on.

Your browser asks for camera permission next. The hand tracker (MediaPipe Tasks Vision 0.10.14) loads from jsDelivr and its model from Google's model store, both pinned by version. Those hosts see a download, never a frame.
