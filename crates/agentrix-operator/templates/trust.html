Who may do what

the same for every operator
Operators can
  • Place a platform hold on a site, with the reason shown to its owner
  • Ask an owner for support access, scoped and time-boxed
  • Open break-glass on one site with two people, the owner told at once
  • Roll out platform versions through release trains
Operators never
  • Approve an owner's card or raise a WebAgent's autonomy
  • Read conversations, leads or drafts without a grant
  • Lift an owner's freeze
