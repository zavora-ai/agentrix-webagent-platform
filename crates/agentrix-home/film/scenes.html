Your website has one job.

Storyboard for the home page film. About 90 seconds, plus a 12-second silent loop for the hero. Warm, flat 2D in the Agentrix colours. The cast are Kilele Bakery's own WebAgent marks. Kilele Bakery is a sample business, and Wanjiru appears only as hands, apron and phone. Every line is on screen as a caption, so the film works on mute.

The hero loop (12 seconds, silent)

Loop · 0:00–0:04

"Your website closes at six."

The shop at night. The website snores on the counter.

Loop · 0:04–0:08

"Your WebAgents don't."

The window warms up. Kilele's WebAgents are at work behind the glass.

Loop · 0:08–0:12

"Upgrade your website into WebAgents."

The brand line, then back to the start. On the page, a play button opens the film.

The film (about 90 seconds)

1 · 0:00–0:06

"This is Kilele Bakery's website. It closes at six."

Slow push in on the shop window, past the CLOSED sign, to the website snoring on the counter.

2 · 0:06–0:13

"It doesn't do Saturdays."

11:04 p.m. A customer's message pops up. The website snores louder. The unread badge sits there.

3 · 0:13–0:21

"Last month it let a stranger in through the back. Didn't say a word."

A beetle in a burglar's mask, with a sack, squeezes through a cat flap marked "plugins". The website doesn't stir.

4 · 0:21–0:30

"And when a customer's AI agent came to order a cake, it got a picture of the menu."

A small robot with an empty basket knocks. The sleeping website prints a blurry photo of the menu. The robot squints, turns it upside down, gives up.

5 · 0:30–0:35

"Wanjiru deserves better."

Morning light, flour on the counter, chai steaming. We see only her apron and hands. Her phone: one message missed at 11:04 p.m. A sigh.

6 · 0:35–0:43

"So she asked Agentrix for a free check-up. No password, nothing to install."

She types her address and taps Show me. The check-up slides in, each finding with its evidence.

7 · 0:43–0:53

"Then everything moves to a new site. Everything except the stranger."

The Agentrix van loads Menu, Cakes, Photos and Prices. The beetle tries to hop into a box; a glass door slides shut. It sulks behind the glass, with a label saying why it was left behind.

8 · 0:53–0:59

"Nothing goes live until she says so."

Her phone: "Your new site is ready. Go live?" Her thumb lands on the fingerprint. A soft click.

9 · 0:59–1:06

"The old site? It waits on standby for 30 days. Just in case."

The old website, now awake, in a coral dressing gown, sips tea on a park bench under a "Standby · 30 days" sign. A pigeon keeps it company.

10 · 1:06–1:12

"Meet her WebAgents."

The marks pop in one by one in Kilele's colours, each with its name, its job and an AI label.

11 · 1:12–1:19

"They answer at 11 p.m., from her own menu."

The same message, the same 11:04 p.m. This time the Concierge answers, honestly, and cites the Cakes page it read.

12 · 1:19–1:27

"They take orders from people, and from their AI agents. And they keep the back door shut."

Left: the robot strolls through a proper door marked "For AI agents" with its order; the Cashier holds it for Wanjiru's OK. Right: the Guardian has padlocked the cat flap; the beetle wanders off, sack empty.

13 · 1:27–1:34

"Every morning they tell her what they did. She decides what matters."

Same counter, same chai, a better morning. The Chief WebAgent's brief in Swahili and English. Her thumb taps Approve on the cake order.

14 · 1:34–1:40

"Upgrade your website into WebAgents."

The brand line and the address box. Small print: Kilele Bakery is a sample business; WebAgents are AI, and say so.
