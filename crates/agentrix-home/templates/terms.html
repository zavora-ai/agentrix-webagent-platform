Terms

Using Agentrix during the pilot

Agentrix is in its pilot, run by Zavora Technologies Ltd. These are the plain terms for using it today. The full subscription terms come with the first paid plan, and you'll see them before you pay. Updated Sun 8 Nov 2026.

Health reports

Anyone can ask for a free health report of a public website. It reads public pages only. It describes what we found, with the evidence; it is not a guarantee that a site is safe.

Moving a site

  • You can move only a site whose domain you prove you control.
  • Nothing goes live until you approve it, and you can undo a go-live for 30 days.
  • Your WebAgents act only within the authority you grant, and every action has a receipt.
  • Your site's content stays yours. You can export it and leave at any time.

The sample sites

Kilele Bakery, Classy Perfumes and Tana Valley Times are invented businesses, marked Sample. Orders there are not real, and payments run in PayPal's sandbox, so no money moves.

The code

The platform is open source under the MIT licence. You can run it yourself.

Questions

Write to Zavora Technologies through zavora.ai.
