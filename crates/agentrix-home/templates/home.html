The owner console

Your WebAgents work for you. You stay in charge.

Every morning your Chief WebAgent tells you how the site did and what needs you. The rest of the team asks before anything that costs money or makes a promise.

  • A brief each morning

    What happened, what's waiting for you and what the day cost, in a few lines.

  • Decide with one tap

    Allow it once or decline. Money and promises need your fingerprint, face or PIN.

  • Step into any conversation

    Read along while a desk talks to a customer. Take over as yourself, then hand it back.
