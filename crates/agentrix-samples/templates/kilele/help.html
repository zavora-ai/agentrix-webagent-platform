What the desks can and can't do

The Front desk, Bread, Cakes, Orders and Visit are AI WebAgents. They answer from this site's pages and show where each answer came from. {{ shell.site.owner }} sets what they may do.

They can

  • Answer questions from this site, with numbered sources you can open.
  • Hold up to {{ grant.max }} loaves for you until {{ grant.until }}, free. You pay at the counter.
  • Design a cake with you from {{ shell.site.name }}'s own options, priced from the price list.
  • Find your order, say where it stands, and pass changes or cancellations to {{ shell.site.owner }}.
  • Refund up to {{ refund.per_order }} an order, at most {{ refund.per_week }} refunds a week, to {{ refund.to }}. The Cashier does this under {{ shell.site.owner }}'s signed rule, valid until {{ refund.until }}. Anything more waits for her.

They can't

  • Ask for a password, a one-time code or card details. They never will.
  • Change your name, email, phone number or address.
  • Charge you or change a price. A payment request appears on your order page only after {{ shell.site.owner }} approves.
  • Hold more than {{ grant.max }} loaves, or hold after {{ grant.until }}, without asking {{ shell.site.owner }}.
  • Promise anything the site doesn't say, such as another delivery area or a cake sooner than its notice.

A desk can never change your details, so nobody can change them through a chat, including someone pretending to be you.
